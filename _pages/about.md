I am an undergraduate at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/) studying Physics, Mathematics, and Applied Mathematics & Statistics, with a minor in Theatre Arts & Studies. I expect to graduate with a B.S. in 2028. Alongside my undergraduate courses I have taken graduate coursework in Quantum Field Theory and in Category Theory with Emily Riehl. I am a Rise Fellow, selected as one of 100 fellows from 80,000 applicants.

I am a research assistant at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center and Johns Hopkins, where I modernized the XAssist analysis pipeline for automated processing of large X-ray datasets, including the full Chandra Source Catalog, and built workflows to identify candidate ultraluminous X-ray sources. I am also a research assistant on the CMS Experiment at CERN, applying machine learning to detect anomalies in high-energy proton–proton collision data.

Earlier, I designed a role-playing game based on cognitive behavioral therapy for children with ADHD, under the supervision of psychiatrist Dr. Nuran Gözpınar, and presented the work at the European Association for Behavioural and Cognitive Therapies Congress 2023.

At Hopkins I am Head of Projects for the Johns Hopkins Data Science Club, and I was previously Social Chair of the Society of Physics Students. On stage, I played R in *Chen's Passage* with JHU Theatre and was an actor in the Witness Theatre I-Show 2026.
